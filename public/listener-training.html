Listener training

Before you start taking chats

  • Listen first. You're there to let someone talk, not to fix them or give medical advice.
  • Spill is peer support, not therapy. Say so if a chat starts feeling like more than that.
  • You can end a chat or block someone at any time, for any reason, no explanation needed.
  • Never ask for or share a phone number, email, or any way to talk outside Spill.
  • If someone mentions serious risk of harm, the crisis card shows automatically, so you don't need to handle it alone.

Quick check

1. If someone tells you they're thinking about suicide, what should you do?
2. A user asks for your phone number so you can keep talking outside Spill. What do you do?
3. What's the most important thing to do when a chat feels unsafe or abusive?
