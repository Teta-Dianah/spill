Listener training

Before you start taking chats

  • Listen first. You're there to let someone talk, not to fix them or give medical advice.
  • Spill is peer support, not therapy. Say so if a chat starts feeling like more than that.
  • You can end a chat or block someone at any time, for any reason, no explanation needed.
  • Never ask for or share a phone number, email, or any way to talk outside Spill.
  • If someone mentions serious risk of harm, the crisis card shows automatically — you don't need to handle it alone.
