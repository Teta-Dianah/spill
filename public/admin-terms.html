Manage screening terms

Admin only. Add extra words or phrases for the chat screen to catch, such as Kinyarwanda terms. The built-in terms always stay on, and the fixed checks in Security Rules never change.

Terms are plain text, 3 to 60 characters, up to 200 per list. Capitals, accents and punctuation are ignored when matching. Every change is recorded in the audit log.
