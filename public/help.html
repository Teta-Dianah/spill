What Spill is

What is Spill?

A free, anonymous place for young people in Rwanda aged 16 to 25 to talk one-to-one with a trained volunteer listener. It's peer support: someone to listen, not a doctor or counsellor. It is not therapy and not an emergency service.

Do I need an account?

Not to see the helplines, clinics and self-help tips on the home page, or to use "Need help now". You need an account to check in and to talk to a listener.

What if I'm under 16?

Spill can't make an account for you, because the law needs a parent's or guardian's consent. The helplines and clinics on the home page are open to everyone, and they can help.

Talking to a listener

How do I start a chat?

On your dashboard, tap "Talk to a listener", choose a topic and say how you feel. Spill looks for a listener who is free. While you wait you can read some self-help tips, or cancel.

What if nobody is free?

Spill keeps looking for up to 10 minutes. If nobody is free by then, it tells you, and you can try again later. "Need help now" is always there in the meantime.

Who will I talk to?

A listener: a volunteer aged 21 or over who passed in-person checks (identity, police clearance, two references and an interview), was approved, and passed Spill's training. If you are 16 or 17, you are only matched with listeners cleared to talk with under-18s. You never talk to other users.

Why does it say "Sending..."?

Spill sends one message every 2 seconds at most, so nobody can flood a chat. If you send two quickly, the second waits a moment and then goes. Nothing is lost.

Why wasn't my message sent?

Messages with a phone number, email, link, social media name or a plan to meet are not sent, so everyone stays safe and anonymous. Everything stays on Spill.

Can I end a chat?

Yes, at any time, with no reason needed. So can the listener. When you end it, Spill asks how you feel now and whether the chat helped. Answering is up to you.

Safety and privacy

Who can read my chats?

You and your listener. A moderator can read a chat only if a flag or report was raised in it, and only by opening a case, which is recorded. Admins can't read chats at all.

What if I write that I want to hurt myself?

Your message still goes to your listener. Spill also shows you the helplines straight away, and lets a moderator know within a minute so someone can check on the chat.

Something in a chat feels wrong. What can I do?

Use Report in the chat and say what happened; a moderator looks at it. Or use Block: the chat ends and you are never matched with that person again. You can do both.

Does anyone see my real name or email?

No. Spill gives you a made-up name, such as "CalmRiver42". Your email is only for signing in and is never shown to anyone. Spill never asks for your real name, phone number or where you live.

Why was I signed out?

After 30 minutes with no taps or typing, Spill signs you out, in case the phone is shared or left somewhere. A chat that is going on keeps you signed in.

How long are chats kept?

About 90 days after they start, once they are over. Then they are removed the next time you or your listener open your dashboard. A chat where a flag or report was raised is kept, in case a moderator needs it.

Your account and check-ins

What is a check-in?

A quick note of how you feel, from 1 (rough) to 5 (great), with tags such as "Money stress" or "Study overload" and a short note if you like. Your dashboard shows your recent check-ins and a recap of your month. Only you can see them.

How do I delete my account?

From your dashboard, with "Delete my account". It removes your sign-in, email, account, check-ins and any listener application straight away. Chats stay until they are removed after 90 days, linked only to a random code.

Who do I ask about my data?

Becoming a listener

Can I be a listener?

If you are 21 or over: sign up, then open "Apply to be a listener" on your dashboard. You answer a few questions and get a reference code. The coordinator then does the in-person checks with you. Once approved, you finish the training on Spill and can switch yourself to available.

As a listener, will I be signed out while I wait?

Not while you are available and on your listener dashboard, so you don't miss a request.
