Listener code of conduct

  • Listen first. You are there to let someone talk, not to fix them or give medical advice.
  • Spill is peer support, not therapy. Say so if a chat starts feeling like more than that.
  • Never ask for or share a phone number, email, or any way to talk outside Spill.
  • What you hear in a chat stays in the chat.
  • You can end a chat or block someone at any time, and so can they.
